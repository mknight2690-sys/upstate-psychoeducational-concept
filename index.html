Evaluation process

1. Inquiry & referral

Parent, counselor, or school contacts the practice. Basic referral questions and documentation needs are reviewed.

2. Records review

Relevant school, medical, and behavioral records are reviewed to guide test selection and focus.

3. Testing sessions

Standardized assessment, behavioral observation, and information gathering from parents and teachers when indicated.

4. Scoring & interpretation

Results are analyzed for diagnosis, eligibility factors, strengths, and areas of need.

5. Written report

A clear written report is prepared with findings and individualized recommendations for home, school, and related services.

6. Feedback & next steps

A feedback session is offered. The report can be used for school meetings, IEP/504 discussions, and external referrals.

Frequently asked questions

Who can refer a child or student for testing?

Parents, guardians, school counselors, and school staff may request an evaluation. Referrals are reviewed before scheduling to confirm the appropriate assessment type.

What is a psychoeducational evaluation?

A structured assessment that examines cognitive ability, academic achievement, attention, behavior, and emotional factors to help explain learning profiles and support educational planning.

Can this evaluation be used for special education?

Yes. The report can provide data useful for eligibility discussions, IEP development, and 504 planning. School districts may still conduct their own evaluations under IDEA/Section 504.

How long does the evaluation take?

Testing and record review vary by case. The practice will provide an estimated timeline during the referral call.

Do you accept insurance?

This practice is private-pay. A detailed receipt can be provided for out-of-network reimbursement inquiries.

What ages do you evaluate?

School-age children, adolescents, and young adults are served based on referral needs and appropriate assessment tools.

Do you accept Union County referral requests?

No. Because the evaluator is employed by Union County schools, private testing is not offered for Union County children. Services are available to families, counselors, and districts outside Union County.
